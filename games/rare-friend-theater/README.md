# Rare Friend Theater

A seven-act interactive comic starring the player's verified Rare Friends Generations NFT. Each Friend's sprite family gives them a stage role and color. Find a hidden star with the spotlight, play its lost lullaby note by note, fold a paper bridge, bring the audience into the story, make a wish, and view a seven-panel comic. Save the rendered image with the browser's image menu.

## Play locally

From the repository root, with Node.js 22+:

```sh
npm ci
npm run dev:game -- games/rare-friend-theater
```

Open the displayed URL, normally `http://localhost:4173`. Connect a browser wallet on Robinhood mainnet (chain 4663) that owns a hardwired Generations NFT of generation 1 or higher. The SDK verifies ownership before loading the game and supplies the selected Friend. No private key, RF funding, or transaction signature is needed for this simulated preview.

In act one, move the spotlight with a mouse or touch and tap the hidden star when it appears. Keyboard players can focus the stage, aim with arrow keys, and press Enter. A "Reveal the clue" button provides a direct alternative. In act three, tap the three glowing notes in order, press Enter or Space on the focused stage, or use "Play next note." In act four, tap the three paper steps in order or use Enter, Space, or "Fold next step." Choose one action per act, then continue. Each choice is kept in the story trail and printed on the PNG comic; the final choice changes its title and finale art. On narrow screens, continuing to a new act returns to the stage. Choose a Moonbeam, Sugarplum, or Mossy backdrop. Use the browser's image menu to save the comic; downloads initiated inside the SDK's sandboxed iframe are blocked. The Backstage Prop Box lets you spend simulated RF on a moon lantern, brass key, or star confetti. The supporter box lets you light 1, 3, or 5 house lights for the same preview balance; those lights appear on stage and in the comic. Replay retains session props, lights, and simulated balance; reloading resets them.

## Economy and implementation

- FriendSDK v0.1.3 supplies wallet connection, fresh ownership verification, selected Friend identity, and sandboxed game runtime. `client.read()` initializes the verified session.
- Each preview session starts with 8 simulated RF. Each prop costs 2 simulated RF. Supporter house lights cost 1, 3, or 5 simulated RF per click, limited by the remaining preview balance. Props and lights have no probabilities, payouts, or resale value. There are no consumables or RF redemption promises.
- The prop and supporter ledgers are local React state, not the SDK's chance-game ledger. No wallet transaction, real donation, or real token spend occurs. A future live implementation would need a reviewed contract action to debit RF from the Friend's canonical wallet, direct actual support to an approved recipient, and persist purchases/support. SDK v0.1.3 does not provide these actions or persistent saves.
- `game.json` contains the SDK's required chance-game schema but its ticket and outcome are unused by this experience and never shown as game mechanics.
- On-chain art reads use FriendSDK's `createFriendReader`; the verified Friend's sprite is drawn into the stage and exported comic. Art reads may require a working Robinhood mainnet RPC.
- All scene art is generated by this project's canvas code; no third-party assets are used.

Build a static preview with `node scripts/dev-game.mjs build games/rare-friend-theater`. The output is `games/rare-friend-theater/.friendsdk/preview/`.
